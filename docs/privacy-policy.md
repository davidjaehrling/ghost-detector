## 2. What stays on your device

- **Camera frames and photos.** When you scan, the app reads the camera image to build the simulated reading and the apparition. This happens on your device. The image is not uploaded, not sent to us and not sent to any ad or analytics service.
- **Sensor values.** The app reads motion and magnetic field sensors to drive the on-screen instrument. These values are used on the device only.
- **Scan history.** Your past scans (time, score, entity profile, and whether you unlocked a Deep Scan) are stored locally on your device. We cannot see them. Removing the app or clearing its data deletes them.
- **Location tags and notes.** Labels you type for a case (for example a room name) are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else, and they are not part of analytics events or shared cards.
- **Field cards.** When you share a field card, the app renders an image on your device and hands it to the share sheet. You choose the app that receives it. We do not receive it.
- **Settings.** Your choices (for example intensity) are stored locally.

## 3. Ads

The app shows ads from Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK: a rewarded video that plays only when you tap DEEP SCAN, an interstitial between scans, and a banner on the history screen.

When the SDK requests an ad, Google and its ad partners may process your device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, and information about the ad interaction. Google acts as an independent controller for this processing. See https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

No ad request is made until the consent step below is finished. If you decline personalised ads, the app can still show ads that are not personalised (limited ads).

## 4. Consent

On first launch, and later whenever the law requires it, the app shows a consent form from Google (User Messaging Platform, a certified IAB TCF consent management platform). In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you choose what may be stored on your device and used for ads. Your choice is stored on the device and re-read at each launch.

You can change your choice at any time: Settings, PRIVACY. This entry reopens the form whenever the law requires it to be available.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and section 25 TDDDG). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future.

## 5. Analytics

If Firebase Analytics is enabled in your build, it starts only after consent covers storing and reading information on your device. Without that consent, nothing is sent.

Only these events are sent:

| Event | Parameters |
|---|---|
| `consent_status` | status of your consent choice |
| `scan_complete` | `tier` (reading tier), `intensity` (your intensity setting) |
| `deep_scan_offered` | none |
| `deep_scan_rewarded` | none |
| `interstitial_shown` | none |
| `share_card` | none |

No photo, image data, scan result text or location is part of these events. Firebase also processes an app instance ID and basic device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, country derived from the IP address) to deliver the service. The advertising ID is not used for analytics in this app. Provider: Google Ireland Limited. See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

## 6. Permissions

- **Camera:** to take the scan image. Frames stay on the device.
- **Vibration:** haptic feedback during a scan. No data is read or sent.
- **Motion and magnetic sensors:** to drive the on-screen instrument. No data is sent. On some devices high sampling rate sensor access is requested for smoother readings.
- **Advertising ID (Google Play services):** declared because the ads SDK can read it. You can reset or delete it in Android settings.
- **Notifications (Android 13 and later):** only if you turn on the field log relay. Notifications are scheduled on your device from the moon phase, the clock and your local log. There is no push service and no push token, and nothing is sent to us. You can turn the relay off in the app settings or in Android settings.
- **Internet:** needed to load ads and to send analytics after consent.

The app does not use the microphone, contacts, files or precise location.

## 7. Children

The app is intended for people aged 16 and over and is not directed at children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
